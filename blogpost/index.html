Optimizing Pop-Up Timing Q1 2025
Pop-up campaigns are targeted messages that appear during user sessions. When timed right, they boost engagement without being intrusive. Today, we'll reveal how to find that perfect timing.
32+ Different Campaigns Engaged
These users aren’t new to pop-ups—they’ve engaged with totaly 32+ different campaigns before. This means they’re proven responders, giving us reliable data.
5,040 Users Participated
To find the ideal timing, we sent the same campaign at 2-minute intervals to randomized users. This isolates time as the only variable.
Engagement Rates
Even this analysis shows the main variable: how many users clicked on the pop-up. 10% of users clicked and joined the campaign
Age Matters
Young users (18–24) were most responsive—but the timing effect held across all age groups. Now, let’s see when they engaged.
The Drop-Off
Initially, most users closed pop-ups instantly when they appeared. But as time passed, this pattern weakened—with a clear dip forming around 14 minutes...
The Rebound
After 16 minutes, closings rise again—but between 14–16 minutes, something remarkable happens.
The Sweet Spot
Here’s our goldilocks zone: 14–16 minutes. Engagement jumps to 35%—a 3.5x lift from baseline.
Key Takeaway
For users who already love campaigns, timing is everything. Send at 14–16 minutes to maximize conversions without increasing frequency.
